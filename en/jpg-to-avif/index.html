JPG to AVIF

Convert JPG images to AVIF in batches, inside your browser.

Convert JPEG images to AVIF straight in your browser, one at a time or all at once. No file is uploaded anywhere: your own computer does the decoding and the encoding, which is why the conversion is instant and there is no size limit.

For photo galleries you already hold as JPEG, AVIF typically halves the weight. Worth serving with a WebP alternative through <picture>.

JPEG is the photographic format everyone has, with lossy compression and universal support. The trade-off is that it has no transparency and degrades a little every time it is saved again. AVIF, on the other hand, is the smallest of the four at equal quality, often half the size of a JPEG, although encoding it is slow and support outside the browser is still patchy.

How to use it

  1. Drag the images onto the area above, or click to pick them.
  2. Choose the target format and, where it applies, the quality.
  3. Press “Process” and download the results one by one or together as a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Is quality lost going from JPEG to AVIF?
AVIF compresses with loss, so yes, a little — controlled by the quality slider. At 80-85 the difference does not show on a screen. Always start from the original so recompressions do not stack up.
What happens to transparency?
JPEG has no transparency, so there is nothing to keep: the result will be opaque either way.
Can I convert a lot at once?
Yes. Drop in as many files as you like: they are processed in parallel across every core your processor has, and you can download them together as a ZIP.
Are the images sent to a server?
No. Everything happens inside this tab. Once the page has loaded you can disconnect from the network and carry on converting.
