JPG to PDF

JPEG photographs into a single PDF, one per page.

Gather JPEG photographs into a single PDF, one per page and in whatever order you like. It is the quick route from phone photos of documents to one file you can send or print.

The JPEGs are embedded as they are, without re-encoding, so the PDF loses nothing against the original photos and is produced quickly.

How to use it

  1. Drag the images in the order you want them.
  2. Choose the page size, orientation and margin.
  3. Press “Combine” and download the PDF.

Frequently asked questions

What order do the pages come out in?
The order of the list, which you can change by dragging the rows.
How do I make every page A4?
Switch the page size to A4. Each photo is scaled to fit whole, centred and keeping its proportions, with whatever margin you set.
Can I mix portrait and landscape photos?
Yes. With orientation on “Follow the image”, each page takes the orientation of its contents, so landscape photos do not end up small in the middle of a portrait sheet.
