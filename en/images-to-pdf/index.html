Images to PDF

A PDF with one image per page, with size and margins under control.

Build a PDF with one image per page. It works both for turning phone photos of documents into a single file, and for packaging a set of screenshots into something you can send and print.

With the “Each image’s own” page size, every page has exactly its photo’s dimensions — no white margins, no scaling. With A4 or Letter, the images are fitted in, centred.

How to use it

  1. Drag the images in the order you want them.
  2. Choose the page size, orientation and margin.
  3. Press “Combine” and download the PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Which image formats does it take?
Anything the browser can open. JPEGs and PNGs are embedded directly; everything else is converted to PNG first.
How do I change the page order?
By dragging the rows in the list, which is the order of the result.
The PDF is huge — what can I do?
Compress or resize the images first. A 12-megapixel photo has far more resolution than any printer will use on an A4 sheet.
