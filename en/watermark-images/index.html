Watermark images

Text on top, in a corner or tiled across the whole photo.

Put a signature or a notice over your photos, in a corner or tiled diagonally across the whole image. In batches, which is where it earns its keep: fifty product shots with the same mark cost the same as one.

The size is given as a percentage of the short side rather than in pixels, so the mark looks the same on a portrait phone photo as on an eight-thousand-pixel panorama.

How to use it

  1. Drag in the photos.
  2. Type the text and choose its position, size and opacity.
  3. Process and download.

Frequently asked questions

Does it actually protect the image?
It deters, which is not the same thing. A mark in the corner is cropped out in a second; one tiled over the image is far more annoying to remove, but not impossible either.
Can I use a logo instead of text?
Not yet — the mark is text. In the meantime you can compose the logo with the text in an editor and treat that as an image.
Why is the mark unreadable on some photos?
Because the colour matches the background. Leave the shadow on, which is exactly what it is for, or pick a colour that contrasts.
